Mugappair Mail

Read more
BUILT TYPE:Commercial 
LOCATION:Chennai 
STATUS:Completed in 2021 

A work space for a neighborhood newspaper in Mogappair, Chennai. The requirement was to rearrange the layout and lift up the space which suits the current work scenario. The workstations and furniture are replaced by lighter and ergonomically oriented pieces in order to reduce the floor and visual chaos. Illumination has been strategically catered to by promoting natural light and incorporating task lamps to eliminate the light clutter.
