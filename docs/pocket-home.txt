In a city like Chennai, owning a home today is no longer just a dream…
it’s a constant negotiation.

Land prices are rising.
Plots are shrinking.
And somewhere in between, aspirations are getting squeezed.

There are typically two paths people take.

One group chooses the comfort of apartments —
a home on the 14th floor, inside a gated community of 1000 units,
packed with amenities… yet often missing something deeply personal.

The other group chooses to build.
They buy a small plot.
They stretch their budget.
They push for a little more space… one more room… one more floor.

But between:
Busy work lives
Lack of clarity
Too many decisions
They end up making compromises… or worse, mistakes that cost them heavily.

And that’s when it hit me.
This is not a creativity problem.
This is a problem that needs solving.

As architects, we often believe we are in the business of design.

But the truth is:
We are not in the creative business.
We are in the problem-solving business.

And this…
this is the problem.
How do you create a meaningful home on a 900 to 1500 sq.ft plot
without making it feel small, compromised, or incomplete?

How do you give someone:
Space
Light
Comfort
Identity
…within constraints that are very real?
That’s where Pocket Home was born.

Pocket Home is not about building smaller houses.
It is about maximising life within compact spaces.

It is about rethinking:
Floor plates
Volumes
Movement
Light
So that a home doesn’t feel like a limitation…
but an experience.

And this is personal.
Because I am one of them.
A millennial who grew up with:
Open terraces
Street-side cricket
Spaces that held memories
And today, we are moving into tighter plots, faster lives, smaller windows.

But does that mean we give up on how a home should feel?
No.

Pocket Home is about bringing that feeling back.
Not through size…
but through design.
Spaces that:
Let light pour in
Allow air to move
Create little moments inside a home
Hold conversations, laughter, and silence
Spaces that create memories.

We design these experiences…
within smaller plots.
With sharper thinking.
With deeper intent.

Because at the end of the day,
👉 A home is not defined by how big it is.
👉 It is defined by how well it lives.
