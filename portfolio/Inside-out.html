Inside Out

Read more
BUILD TYPE:  Residential
LOCATION:  Chennai
STATUS:  Proposed

The Inside Out residence was developed in a 1200 sqft plot, where the client had a simple brief which included two large bedrooms in the first floor and a compact guest room in the ground floor apart from the other core requirements of the residence. The design idea was initiated with a presence of the community park in the rear side of the building. This shaped the design process of the project by opening up the balconies on the rear and the front side of the plot. A double height space was also incorporated in the project.
